# Privacy Policy — Locket Album

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Locket Album is a photo gallery for Android. It works only on your phone.

## The short version

**Locket Album does not collect, send, or share any of your data.** It has no internet
permission, so Android itself prevents it from connecting to the internet. There are no
accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

## What the app accesses, and why

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Photos and videos | To show your photos and videos, and to edit, star, trash or restore them when you ask. |
| Photo location (`ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION`) | To show where a photo was taken in its Details panel. It is never sent anywhere. |
| Media management (optional) | If you turn it on, starring and trashing skip Android's confirmation each time. |
| Set wallpaper | To set a photo as your home or lock screen wallpaper when you ask. |

## What the app stores

The albums and tags you create, and small fingerprints used to stack similar photos (if you
turn that on), are kept in a database inside the app on your phone. Nobody else can see them,
including us. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Your photos themselves stay where they are in
your phone's storage.

## Sharing

The app only shares a photo when you tap Share. It then hands the photo to the app you pick,
and that app's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
